## How impact is documented

Citation totals alone do not explain research influence. For petition or professional-review purposes, the supporting record should also identify the independent works that cite a publication, the institutions represented by those authors, and how the cited method or finding was used. Those details are maintained as a dated evidence record and are added publicly only when they can be verified from the citing source.
